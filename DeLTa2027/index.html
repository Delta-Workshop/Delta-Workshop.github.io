About DeLTa

From theory to principles for generation

Deep generative models support image and audio synthesis, language generation, and scientific modeling. Their usefulness depends on how they learn a distribution and how that learned distribution changes during sampling, acceleration, and adaptation.

The proposed third DeLTa workshop brings together researchers in learning theory, probability, dynamical systems, statistical physics, and applied generative modeling. Building on the 2025 and 2026 editions, we aim to connect mathematical understanding with practical decisions in generative modeling.

Our central question

Which properties of learning and sampling dynamics provide actionable principles for efficient and reliable generation?

Scientific scope

Three connected questions

01

Learning structure

How do generative models learn structure and generalize?

Learning dynamics, data geometry, representation learning, memorization, and statistical physics of generation.

02

Efficient sampling

What is preserved when sampling is accelerated?

Diffusion and flow matching, numerical methods, stochastic dynamics, few-step generation, and distillation.

03

Reliable control

How can guidance and adaptation control a learned distribution reliably?

Guidance, constraints, post-training, robustness, and evaluation in scientific and multimodal applications.

The scope spans continuous and discrete generative models, with connections to scientific discovery, audio, biology, and trustworthy AI.
